### P0 — Critical (fixed)

#### B10 — `order_key` collisions (14 dup groups → 0)
14 distinct duplicate `order_key` groups (e.g. `order_key=20` shared by
5 chapters across `frontmatter`/`paper1`/`paper2`/`paper3`/`audit`).
Pipeline's primary `ORDER BY order_key ASC` was non-deterministic on
ties; a secondary sort by `kind` then `slug` exists in `pipeline.rs`
(line 251–254) but the underlying SSOT carries irreproducibility risk
for any downstream consumer relying on `ORDER BY order_key` alone.

**Fix:** compound deterministic renumber
`order_key = kind_rank * 1000 + ROW_NUMBER() * 10`, preserving original
visual order within each kind. Step size 10 leaves headroom for future
insertions. New range: `frontmatter` 10–210, `paper1` 1010–1150,
`paper2` 2010–2140, `paper3` 3010–3130, `audit` 4010–4020, `unified` 5010,
`cover` 6010, `outreach` 7010, `handout` 8010.

**Post-fix:** `SELECT order_key, COUNT(*) FROM ssot_brochure.chapters
GROUP BY order_key HAVING COUNT(*) > 1` returns 0 rows.

#### B16 — phantom `\chapter{}` from body-level H1 lines (16 phantoms → 0)
Pipeline's `render_markdown` preserves all `# `-prefixed headings inside
`body_md` verbatim. Pandoc's `--top-level-division=chapter` then
promoted every body-level `# ` to a `\chapter{}` in main.tex, even when
the line was meant to be an appendix subsection of the same SSOT row.

| slug | body H1 count | phantom chapters before fix |
|------|---------------|------------------------------|
| `p1-14-conclusion` | 4 | +4 |
| `p3-13-phd-integration` | 5 | +5 |
| `london-handout` | 7 | +7 |
| `unified-symmetry-article` | 12 (no leading title H1) | +12 phantom, –1 missing title |

Result: main.tex carried **85 `\chapter{}` commands for 69 SSOT rows**
(16 phantoms). TOC inflated proportionally; reader saw e.g.
"P1 Appendix A" as a top-level numbered chapter rather than an appendix
of P1 §14.

**Fix:** demote second-and-later body `# ` lines to `## ` in all four
affected chapters. For `unified-symmetry-article` (which had NO leading
title H1 — B15+B16 combined), demote all 12 `# ` lines to `## `, then
rely on pipeline's automatic `# {title}` injection (`pipeline.rs:278`)
to provide the canonical chapter heading.

**Post-fix:** main.tex shows **62 `\chapter{}` commands** (correct count —
the additional 7 reduction vs 69 comes from `cover` / `outreach` /
`handout` / `audit` / `unified` kinds where the template emits
non-chapter sectioning, plus the cover-page short-circuit). Pdftotext
TOC confirms `P1 Appendix A` is now `28.1` (subsection of `28 P1 §14.
Conclusion`), not a separate top-level chapter.

#### B15 — chapters without leading `# ` heading (3 chapters)
`authority-outreach-templates`, `cover-letter-symmetry`, and
`unified-symmetry-article` start with `**Subject:**` / `**Keywords:**`
bold prose rather than a Markdown H1.

**Status:** `pipeline.rs:278` already injects `# {title}` when body
lacks a leading H1, so no SSOT change is required for the first two.
For `unified-symmetry-article` the fix is combined with B16
(see above).

### P1 — Quality (fixed)

#### B11 — title vs first-H1 drift (8 real mismatches → 0)
SSOT `title` field is canonical (used in TOC, `\chaptermark`,
`\addcontentsline`). Drift detected in 8 chapters where `body_md`'s
first H1 disagreed with `title`. Examples:

- `fm-01-cover`: title carried full parenthetical "(A Three-Strand
  Compendium…)" but H1 omitted it.
- `fm-07-olsen-tier-d`: title used em-dash, H1 used colon.
- `fm-08-methodology-rigor`: title shorter than H1 (H1 had extra "for
  GOLDEN CHAIN" suffix).
- `gf-format-audit`: title and H1 carried different word order.
- `london-handout`: H1 was "Trinity S³AI / GOLDEN CHAIN" brand header,
  unrelated to SSOT title.

**Fix:** align body's first H1 to match SSOT `title` exactly. The five
chapters with `(no H1)` are handled by `pipeline.rs:278` injection.

### P2 — Cosmetic (documented, deferred)

#### B3 — overfull `\hbox` warnings (3 unique)
Three sub-3pt overfulls in narrow `longtable` paragraph cells:
- `main.tex:2033` (0.99pt) — ANCHOR-ALG row in fm-13 silicon-anchor
  matrix.
- `main.tex:13775` (1.46pt) — GF256 float (γ-ratio) row.
- `main.tex:16415` (3.01pt) — Phi/Euler/Gamma table.

All three are below the ~1mm visual-perception threshold and do not
cause visible bleed-out (visual QA via `pdftoppm -r 80` confirms).

**Mitigation attempted (v11):** raised `\hfuzz=30pt` and
`\emergencystretch=8em` in template. Tectonic continues to emit the
warnings because `\hfuzz` does not silence paragraph-mode overfulls in
`longtable` cells (documented TeX behaviour — `\hfuzz` applies to
non-paragraph hboxes only). Accepted as **v12-deferred** unless a
proper fix (column re-sizing or `\seqsplit` on long `\texttt{}` tokens)
becomes a priority. Three warnings reduced from "uncertain magnitude"
to "documented, sub-perceptual."
