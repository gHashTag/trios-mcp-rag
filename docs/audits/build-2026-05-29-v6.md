## Known limitations (cosmetic, not functional)

1. **Unicode superscript `³` renders as `3 ` (with space) in body text** — pandoc/tectonic limitation with `latinmodern-math.otf`. URLs and PDF links are correct; only flat-text extraction shows the space. Fix would require switching to a font with proper math-text fallback or rewriting all `S³AI` as `S\textsuperscript{3}AI` in raw LaTeX. Deferred.
2. **`pdftotext -layout` breaks long URLs across lines** — gives the impression of "broken URLs" in text dumps. The PDF hyperlinks themselves are intact and clickable.
3. **TOC entry "α       −1"** — pandoc adds excess spacing between α and the superscript when wrapped by `longtable`. Cosmetic.
